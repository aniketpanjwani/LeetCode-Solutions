# Source Capsule Template

Use this only when you provide an outside source that might shape prep strategy. Capsules are weak, provenance-tagged signals; they do not change the curriculum unless you explicitly incorporate one.

Agents should not research, auto-populate, or seed this file. If you paste a Reddit thread, blog post, hiring note, or other advice source, capture the claim here and keep the uncertainty visible.

## Schema

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `source` | URL, title, author, or enough context to recover the source later. |
| `date` | Date the source was published or captured. |
| `claim` | The specific advice or observation, stated narrowly. |
| `confidence` | Low, medium, or high confidence, with a short reason if useful. |
| `applicability` | Why this may or may not apply to ARENA or later job-search prep. |
| `conflicts` | Known tensions with repo evidence, review-log data, or other advice. |
| `action` | What, if anything, should change because of this source. |

## Blank Template

```yaml
source: "<URL or source description>"
date: "<YYYY-MM-DD>"
claim: "<narrow claim>"
confidence: "<low | medium | high>"
applicability: "<where this advice applies>"
conflicts: "<known conflicts or none>"
action: "<no action | watch | discuss | incorporate after explicit approval>"
```
